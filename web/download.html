Download for Windows Free · Windows 10 and 11

Windows

Easiest

One installer with everything inside (about 180 MB). Updates itself.

  1. Open PapiClips-Setup.exe.
  2. If Windows says "protected your PC": More info, then Run anyway.
  3. Press Install. Open it from the PapiClips icon on your desktop.
Download for Windows

Mac

A zip with PapiClips inside. The first start sets itself up (5 to 10 minutes).

  1. Double-click the zip to unpack it, and put the folder in your home folder.
  2. Right-click Start-PapiClips-Mac.command, then Open, then Open again (only the first time).
  3. PapiClips opens in your browser.
Download for Mac

Linux

The same zip as for Mac. Needs Python 3.10 or newer and ffmpeg.

  1. Unpack the zip.
  2. In a terminal, in that folder: bash start-linux.sh
  3. Open http://127.0.0.1:8765 if your browser doesn't open by itself.
